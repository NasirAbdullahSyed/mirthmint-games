ORBIT BREAKER

Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026

Mirthmint Games publishes and operates Orbit Breaker. This policy explains how the Android game and this support website handle information. For privacy questions or requests, contact mirthmintgames@gmail.com.

Playing and saving on your device

The game stores settings, local best scores, unfinished runs and queued score submissions on your device. You can play offline without signing in. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes its local data; this does not remove information already submitted to online services.

Advertising and privacy choices

Ad-enabled builds use Google AdMob and Unity Ads through AdMob mediation. These services may process your IP address and approximate location, advertising and other device identifiers, device/app information, ad interactions and performance diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and support their services. Orbit does not request GPS location permission. Advertising partners may combine data under their own policies.

Where required, the game presents a consent message before eligible ad requests. You can accept, refuse or manage the available purposes and partners. Reopen Settings → Privacy choices when available to change your decision. Withdrawing consent affects future processing; it does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Depending on your location and choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, limited or unavailable. An optional rewarded continue requires your choice to watch an ad.

For information about the providers and their controls, see Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps, and Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. The consent message identifies additional advertising vendors where applicable. You can also use Android's advertising-ID controls.

Optional online rankings

When online rankings are enabled in your build and you choose to sign in and submit a score, Google Play Games supplies a player identifier and display name. We use these to associate submissions with your profile. Your display name, score and rank are visible on the leaderboard. We do not receive your Google password.

The ranking service processes your gameplay replay to verify the score and stores player/profile records, verified run summaries, best scores and submission times for rankings and abuse prevention. Replay content is processed rather than stored as a replay archive. A session token is stored on your device to authenticate requests. Backend hosting, including Supabase when used, processes network and request information to operate and secure the service. Builds without a configured online service do not submit these records to our ranking service.

Why information is processed and who receives it

We use information to provide the game and requested rankings, save progress, show and measure advertising, prevent invalid scores or fraud, respond to support requests and meet applicable obligations. For users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, processing relies on consent where required, providing requested services, or legitimate interests such as service security where applicable. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Recipients include Google, Unity, advertising vendors identified in the consent interface, and service providers that host the website, ranking service or support email. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. Advertising processing may count as a sale or sharing of personal information under some US state laws. Use the available privacy-choice interface to opt out, or contact us for assistance.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until it is removed by you or replaced by the game. The ranking service is designed to prune recent run summaries older than 30 days and rate-limit submission records older than one day during subsequent submissions; cleanup may not occur immediately if there are no submissions. Player profiles and all-time best scores have no automatic expiry and remain until removed or the service is retired. Session tokens normally expire after 30 days.

To request access, correction or deletion of your ranking/profile data, email the contact above with your Play Games display name and enough information to identify the record. Do not send passwords or authentication codes. We may request information necessary to verify ownership. Clearing local data alone does not remove server records, and deleting an Orbit record does not delete your Google account. Advertising and hosting providers retain information under their own policies. Support correspondence is kept as necessary to address the request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

International processing and your rights

The publisher is based in Pakistan. Google, Unity, GitHub and hosting providers may process information in other countries under their applicable transfer arrangements and privacy policies. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of sale/sharing or targeted advertising. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. EEA/UK users may also complain to their local data-protection authority. We do not deny the core offline game because you make a privacy request.

Children

Orbit Breaker is a general-audience game and is not primarily directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact us. Age and consent treatment must follow the applicable settings and requirements for the distributed version.

This website and security

This static website uses GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising, contact forms or tracking scripts to the site. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Contacting us by email shares the information you send with us and the email provider. Please include only what is needed.

We use access controls and encrypted network transport where supported by our services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the game's data practices change and revise the effective date. Material changes will be communicated in the app or through an appropriate notice when required.
