How can we help?

Email nasirabdullahsyed@gmail.com for support, privacy questions or a request to access, correct or delete ranking/profile data.

Report a game issue

Include your game version, phone model, Android version and the steps that led to the issue. Send screenshots only if they are useful, and remove private information first.

Request data removal

Use the subject “Orbit Breaker data request” and include your Play Games display name and the relevant score if available. We may need to verify ownership before acting. Never send your Google password, authentication codes or session tokens.

To remove local saves and settings, clear Orbit Breaker's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. This does not remove already submitted server records or your Google account.
